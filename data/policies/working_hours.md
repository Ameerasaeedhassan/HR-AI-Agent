# DemoCo Working Hours and Attendance Policy

Policy ID: WH
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Human Resources
Approved By: Head of Human Resources
Review Date: 2027-01-01

Status: Fictional policy for an educational HR Copilot project.
The rules below are invented company rules, not a statement of
employment law.

## WH-01: Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the standard working schedule,
attendance expectations, and approval procedures for
changes to working arrangements.

It applies to full-time employees on the standard office
schedule.

Shift workers, part-time employees, interns, and contractors
must confirm their schedules with HR. The standard schedule
must not be assumed to apply to them.

## WH-02: Standard Working Schedule

The standard working week is Monday through Friday.

Office hours are 09:00 to 18:00, with a one-hour unpaid
break from 13:00 to 14:00.

This represents eight working hours per day and
40 working hours per week.

Saturdays and Sundays are non-working days for employees
on this schedule.

All times refer to the employee's assigned office location.

## WH-03: Breaks and Availability

Employees must take their scheduled break and coordinate
coverage where their role requires continuous service.

Changes to the break time require line-manager approval
and do not change the total daily working hours.

During working hours, employees must remain reasonably
available through the company's agreed communication channels.

A full working day is eight working hours.
A half working day is four working hours, excluding breaks.

## WH-04: Attendance Records

Employees must record their actual start and finish times
in the attendance system.

Employees must not record attendance on behalf of another
person.

If a record is missing or incorrect, the employee must
submit a correction request within two working days of
discovering the issue.

Attendance corrections require line-manager verification.

## WH-05: Late Arrival and Unexpected Absence

Employees who expect to arrive late must notify their
line manager as soon as possible and provide an estimated
arrival time.

Reporting a delay does not automatically authorize a
schedule change.

Unexpected absence must be reported before the scheduled
starting time where reasonably possible.

Sickness-related absence is handled under the Sick Leave
Policy, including its notification and documentation rules.

## WH-06: Flexible Working Hours

Employees may request a regular start time between
08:00 and 10:00.

An approved flexible schedule must retain eight working
hours and a one-hour unpaid break per day.

For example, an approved 08:00 start normally corresponds
to a 17:00 finish with a one-hour unpaid break.

Approval must be recorded by the line manager and HR.
Employees must follow the standard schedule until approval
is confirmed.

Flexible hours do not change annual or sick leave entitlement.

## WH-07: Remote Working

Remote working is not an automatic entitlement.

Employees must obtain written line-manager approval for
the requested dates before working remotely.

Approved remote-working days count as working days,
not leave days.

Employees must follow their approved hours, remain
available, and use company-approved systems.

Requests to work from another country require separate
HR and IT review and are not authorized by ordinary
remote-working approval.

## WH-08: Additional Hours

Employees must obtain written line-manager approval
before performing additional hours beyond their
approved schedule.

Attendance records alone do not establish entitlement
to additional pay or time off.

This policy does not specify overtime payment rates
or compensatory leave calculations. Such questions
must be referred to HR and Payroll.

## WH-09: Public Holidays and Temporary Schedules

HR maintains the approved public-holiday calendar.

Company-recognized public holidays are excluded from
annual and sick leave deductions for employees on
the standard schedule.

Temporary changes to office hours must be communicated
through a formal HR notice stating the applicable
dates and employee groups.

The HR Copilot must not invent holiday dates or
temporary schedules when no approved record is available.

## WH-10: Exceptions and Related Policies

Permanent schedule changes require written approval
from HR and the line manager.

Any exception must identify the employee or group,
the approved arrangement, and its effective dates.

Annual leave requests follow the Annual Leave Policy.
Sickness-related absence follows the Sick Leave Policy.

This policy does not define disciplinary penalties,
salary deductions, or special working-hour entitlements.
Questions on these matters must be referred to HR.
