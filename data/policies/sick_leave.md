# DemoCo Sick Leave Policy

Policy ID: SL
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Human Resources
Approved By: Head of Human Resources
Review Date: 2027-01-01

Status: Fictional policy for an educational HR Copilot project.
The rules below are invented company rules, not a statement of
employment law.

## SL-01: Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how eligible employees report sickness,
provide supporting documents, and record sick leave.

It applies to full-time employees working the standard
Monday-to-Friday schedule, including employees on probation.

Part-time employees, interns, and contractors must confirm
their arrangements with HR.

## SL-02: Annual Entitlement

Eligible employees receive up to 10 paid working days of
sick leave per calendar year.

The full allowance is available from the joining date.
It is not prorated or accrued monthly.

Unused sick leave does not carry over into the following year
and cannot be exchanged for cash or annual leave.

Absence beyond the allowance requires HR review.
Additional paid or unpaid leave is not automatically granted.

## SL-03: Reporting an Absence

Employees must notify their line manager before their
scheduled starting time on the first day of absence.

If an emergency prevents timely notification, the employee
or a representative must contact the manager as soon as
reasonably possible.

The notification should include the expected duration of
absence, if known. Employees are not required to share
their diagnosis with their manager.

Employees must update the manager if the expected return
date changes.

## SL-04: Recording the Request

Employees must submit a sick leave request through the
HR system within two working days of returning to work.

The request must include the absence dates and any required
supporting documentation.

If the employee cannot access the system, HR may record
the request on their behalf.

## SL-05: Supporting Documents

Absences lasting one or two consecutive scheduled working
days may be self-certified through the HR system.

Absences lasting three or more consecutive scheduled working
days require a medical certificate covering the absence.

Weekends and company-recognized public holidays do not count
toward this threshold and do not break an otherwise
consecutive absence.

For example, absence on Friday, Monday, and Tuesday requires
a medical certificate when all three are scheduled working
days and none is a company-recognized public holiday.

Missing documentation must be referred to HR for review.
The absence is not automatically converted to annual leave.

## SL-06: Counting Sick Leave Days

Sick leave is counted in scheduled working days.

For the standard Monday-to-Friday schedule, Saturdays,
Sundays, and company-recognized public holidays are excluded.

Sick leave may be recorded in half-day or full-day increments.

An absence spanning two calendar years is allocated to
each year according to the working days falling within it.

## SL-07: Verification and Balance

The line manager acknowledges the absence notification.
HR verifies eligibility, documentation, and the remaining
sick leave allowance.

The remaining allowance is calculated as:

Remaining sick leave =
10 days - verified paid sick leave taken in that calendar year

Pending requests must be shown separately and are not
treated as verified paid leave until HR completes its review.

Requests exceeding the remaining allowance must be
referred to HR. The standard tool must not report a
negative paid sick leave balance.

## SL-08: Illness During Annual Leave

Employees who become ill during approved annual leave may
request that eligible days be reclassified as sick leave.

Reclassification requires:
- A medical certificate covering the requested dates.
- Sufficient remaining sick leave allowance.
- HR verification and approval.

A medical certificate is required for reclassification
regardless of the number of days requested.

Only scheduled working days may be reclassified.
Approved reclassification restores the corresponding
annual leave days without counting the same absence twice.

## SL-09: Medical Information and Confidentiality

Medical certificates must be submitted through the
designated HR channel.

Access to medical documents is restricted to authorized
HR personnel.

Managers receive only information needed for attendance,
work arrangements, and operational coverage.

This policy does not authorize storing diagnoses or medical
certificates in the HR Copilot's employee analytics dataset.

## SL-10: Return to Work and Exceptions

Employees should confirm their return date with the
line manager.

Any recommended workplace adjustments must be coordinated
with HR through the designated process.

Disputed records and exceptional circumstances require
HR review. The employee must not assume an exception
has been approved.

This policy does not define maternity leave, workplace
injury benefits, or medical insurance coverage.
Questions on those topics must be referred to HR or
the relevant policy.
