# DemoCo Probation and Confirmation Policy

Policy ID: PR
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Human Resources
Approved By: Head of Human Resources
Review Date: 2027-01-01

Status: Fictional policy for an educational HR Copilot project.
The rules below are invented company rules, not a statement of
employment law.

## PR-01: Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the onboarding, performance review,
and confirmation process for newly hired full-time employees.

It does not apply to interns, contractors, or part-time
employees. Their arrangements must be confirmed with HR.

## PR-02: Standard Probation Period

The standard probation period is three calendar months
from the employee's joining date.

The scheduled review date is calculated by adding three
calendar months to the joining date. If the target month
does not contain the same day number, its last day is used.

For example, an employee joining on January 15 has a
scheduled review date of April 15.

Reaching the scheduled review date does not automatically
confirm employment or activate confirmation-dependent benefits.

## PR-03: Onboarding and Objectives

During the first five working days, the line manager
must explain the employee's responsibilities, performance
objectives, and required training.

Objectives must be documented and shared with the employee.

Employees must complete assigned onboarding activities
and raise any access, equipment, or training issues
with their manager.

Role-required equipment may be provided during probation,
as described in the Employee Benefits Policy.

## PR-04: Progress Reviews

The line manager should conduct a progress discussion
approximately one month after joining.

A final probation review should take place before
the scheduled review date.

Reviews consider:
- Progress against agreed objectives.
- Quality and reliability of work.
- Collaboration and communication.
- Completion of required training.
- Attendance records, considered with approved leave.

Managers must document feedback and give employees
an opportunity to respond.

## PR-05: Confirmation Decision

The line manager submits a recommendation to HR.

HR verifies the review and issues the written outcome.

An employee is confirmed only when HR records an approved
confirmation with an explicit effective date.

A manager's verbal statement alone is not confirmation.

The HR record must include the decision, effective date,
approver, and supporting review reference.

## PR-06: Pending or Delayed Reviews

If the review is delayed, the employee or manager should
contact HR.

A pending review must not be treated as either successful
confirmation or unsuccessful completion.

The HR Copilot must use the recorded status and must not
infer confirmation from elapsed service alone.

This policy does not establish an automatic probation
extension. Any proposed change requires HR review and
separate written documentation.

## PR-07: Annual Leave During Probation

Annual leave accrues from the joining date according
to the Annual Leave Policy.

Employees on probation may request accrued annual leave
through the standard approval process.

Requests remain subject to available balance,
notice requirements, and manager approval.

Probation status alone does not create additional
annual leave entitlement.

## PR-08: Sick Leave During Probation

Full-time employees on probation are covered by the
Sick Leave Policy.

The annual sick leave allowance, notification rules,
and medical certificate requirements apply.

Probation status does not remove the requirement
to report absence or provide supporting documents.

## PR-09: Benefits After Confirmation

Learning and wellbeing allowances become available
from the effective confirmation date recorded by HR,
subject to the Employee Benefits Policy.

A scheduled review date or pending recommendation
does not activate these allowances.

Confirmation does not automatically guarantee a
salary increase, bonus, or promotion.

Any compensation change requires separate written approval.

## PR-10: Other Outcomes and Questions

If successful confirmation is not recommended,
HR must communicate the outcome and applicable next steps
through the appropriate process.

This policy does not define termination procedures,
notice periods, final settlements, or appeal deadlines.

The HR Copilot must not invent these rules.
Questions about them must be referred to HR or
an applicable approved document.

Employees who believe their status is incorrect should
ask HR to verify the official record.
