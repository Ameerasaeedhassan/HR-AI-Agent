# DemoCo Employee Benefits Policy

Policy ID: BN
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Human Resources
Approved By: Head of Human Resources
Review Date: 2027-01-01

Status: Fictional policy for an educational HR Copilot project.
The rules below are invented company rules, not a statement of
employment law.

## BN-01: Purpose and Scope

This policy describes DemoCo's learning allowance,
wellbeing reimbursement, and company equipment arrangements.

It applies to full-time employees.

Part-time employees, interns, and contractors must confirm
their benefits with HR. Eligibility must not be assumed.

Benefits are subject to the conditions in each section.
An available allowance does not guarantee approval.

## BN-02: Learning and Development Allowance

Full-time employees who have successfully completed
probation may claim up to AED 2,000 per calendar year
for approved professional development.

Eligible expenses include job-related courses,
certification examinations, and professional workshops.

The allowance becomes available on the effective date
of probation confirmation recorded by HR.

The annual limit is not prorated in the confirmation year.

Unused allowance does not carry over and cannot be
exchanged for cash.

## BN-03: Learning Approval Process

Employees must obtain written approval from their
line manager and HR before enrolling or making payment.

Requests must include:
- The course or examination title.
- The provider.
- The business relevance.
- The total cost.
- The expected completion date.

Approval must specify the maximum reimbursable amount.

Expenses paid before approval are not eligible under
the standard process. Exceptions require written
approval from the Head of HR.

## BN-04: Learning Reimbursement

Employees must submit a claim within 30 calendar days
after completing the approved activity.

The claim must include the approval, an itemized receipt,
proof of payment, and evidence of attendance or completion.

Reimbursement is limited to the lowest of:
- The eligible amount actually paid.
- The approved maximum amount.
- The remaining annual learning allowance.

The expense is allocated to the calendar year in which
the activity is completed.

Pending approved claims reserve the corresponding
allowance. Paid claims must not be deducted twice.

Refunds received from the provider must be reported
to HR so the claim and allowance can be corrected.

## BN-05: Wellbeing Allowance

Full-time employees who have successfully completed
probation may claim up to AED 600 per calendar year
for eligible wellbeing expenses.

Eligible expenses are gym memberships and organized
fitness classes purchased after probation confirmation.

Equipment, clothing, food, supplements, and medical
treatment are not covered by this allowance.

The annual limit is not prorated in the confirmation year.

Unused allowance does not carry over and has no cash value.

## BN-06: Wellbeing Claims

Claims require an itemized receipt and proof of payment.

Employees must submit claims within 30 calendar days
of payment. HR verifies eligibility before reimbursement.

The expense is allocated to the calendar year of payment,
including when a membership extends into the next year.

Reimbursement cannot exceed the eligible amount paid
or the remaining annual wellbeing allowance.

Learning and wellbeing allowances are separate.
Unused funds cannot be transferred between them.

## BN-07: Company Equipment

Company equipment is assigned according to role
requirements and manager approval, including during
probation where needed.

Equipment remains company property and must be recorded
in the asset register.

Employees must report loss, theft, or damage to IT
and their line manager as soon as possible.

Equipment must be returned when requested or when
employment ends.

This policy does not define employee liability or
salary deductions for lost or damaged equipment.

## BN-08: Processing and Payment

HR reviews complete reimbursement claims within
10 working days of submission.

Requests for missing information pause this review period.

Approved claims are scheduled for the next available
payroll cycle, subject to the payroll cutoff.

Submitting a claim does not mean it has been approved.

Employees may contact HR to check the status of a claim.

## BN-09: Exclusions and Missing Information

This policy does not define:
- Medical insurance coverage.
- Housing or transport allowances.
- Flight tickets or travel benefits.
- Relocation assistance.
- Bonuses.
- End-of-service payments.

The HR Copilot must not infer entitlement to these
benefits from this document.

Questions about excluded benefits must be referred
to HR or the relevant policy.

## BN-10: Records and Exceptions

HR maintains the official benefit eligibility and
allowance records.

Claims must not be reimbursed more than once.
An expense cannot be claimed under both allowances.

Exceptions require written approval from the Head of HR,
including the reason, amount, and applicable dates.

An exception for one employee does not automatically
change the rules for other employees.
