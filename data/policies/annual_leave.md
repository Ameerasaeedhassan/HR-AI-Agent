# DemoCo Annual Leave Policy

Policy ID: AL
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Human Resources
Approved By: Head of Human Resources
Review Date: 2027-01-01

Status: Fictional policy for an educational HR Copilot project.
The rules below are invented company rules, not a statement of
employment law.

## AL-01: Purpose and Scope

This policy explains annual leave entitlement, accrual,
approval, and recordkeeping at DemoCo.

It applies to full-time employees working the standard
Monday-to-Friday schedule.

Part-time employees, interns, and contractors are outside
the scope of this policy. Their arrangements must be
confirmed with Human Resources.

## AL-02: Entitlement and Accrual

Eligible employees receive 25 working days of annual leave
for a complete calendar year of service.

The leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.

Leave accrues from the employee's joining date at a rate
of 25/12 days per complete calendar month of service.
A partial calendar month does not earn leave under this
fictional policy.

Accrual calculations retain their full precision.
Balances are displayed to two decimal places.

Leave may be requested in half-day or full-day increments.
Displayed rounding does not create additional entitlement.

## AL-03: Submitting a Request

Employees must submit requests through the HR system at
least 10 calendar days before the proposed start date.

Each request must include:
- The requested start and end dates.
- The number of working days requested.
- A brief handover plan.
- The name of the proposed colleague covering urgent work,
  where applicable.

Employees should avoid making non-refundable travel
arrangements before receiving approval.

Urgent requests with shorter notice may be considered by
the line manager and HR, but approval is not guaranteed.

## AL-04: Approval and Operational Coverage

All annual leave requires line-manager approval.

Managers consider available leave balance, staffing levels,
overlapping requests, operational commitments, and handover
arrangements.

Having sufficient leave balance does not guarantee approval.

Managers should respond within five working days.
A request remains pending until explicitly approved;
a delayed response does not count as approval.

Employees must not begin leave while a request is pending
or rejected.

## AL-05: Balance Calculation and Deductions

The available balance is calculated as:

Available balance =
accrued current-year entitlement
+ valid unused carryover
- current-year leave already taken
- approved future leave

Approved future leave is reserved immediately so that the
same balance cannot be used for another request.

When approved leave is taken, it moves from the reserved
amount to the taken amount and is not deducted twice.

Pending and rejected requests do not reduce the balance.

Standard requests cannot exceed the available balance.
Requests to use unaccrued leave require separate written
approval from HR and the line manager and are outside the
standard calculation process.

## AL-06: Counting Leave Days

Annual leave is counted in working days.

For the standard Monday-to-Friday schedule, Saturdays and
Sundays are excluded.

Dates listed in DemoCo's approved public-holiday calendar
are also excluded.

For example, leave from Monday through the following
Sunday counts as five working days when no public holiday
falls within that period.

## AL-07: Carryover and Expiry

Employees may carry over up to five unused accrued days
into the next calendar year.

Carryover is recorded by HR after the year-end balance
review. Days already taken or committed to approved future
leave cannot also be carried over.

Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the new year.
They are used before current-year entitlement.

Any unused carryover expires after March 31 unless the
Head of HR approves a written exception before that date.

Unused days above the five-day limit expire at year-end
unless a written exception has been approved.

## AL-08: Changes and Cancellations

Employees must request changes or cancellations through
the HR system.

Changes to approved dates require fresh manager approval.

When a cancellation is approved, the associated reserved
days are released back to the available balance.

If leave is shortened after it has started, HR must verify
the actual return-to-work date before restoring unused days.

Expired carryover is not automatically restored through
a cancellation. HR must review such cases.

## AL-09: Handover and Return to Work

Before leave begins, employees must update outstanding
tasks, share necessary handover information, and activate
an out-of-office message identifying an appropriate contact.

Employees must return on their next scheduled working day
after the approved leave ends.

If an unexpected event prevents their return, they must
notify the line manager and HR as soon as reasonably possible.
An extension requires approval.

## AL-10: Records, Disputes, and Exceptions

HR maintains the official leave records.

Employees who identify an incorrect balance should contact
HR with the relevant request details. The disputed balance
must be verified before additional leave is confirmed.

Exceptions must be documented with the approver's name,
approval date, and reason.

This policy does not define sick leave, unpaid leave,
leave encashment, or final-settlement calculations.
Questions on these topics must be referred to the
relevant policy or HR.
